Every finding passes five checks

1 · PHYSICS

Independent methods

Water index, ESA's own classification and a local threshold must agree; the spread is the error bar.

2 · GROUND TRUTH

Official records

Lake area must track the USBR gauge; burn maps must match NIFC fire perimeters.

3 · AUDITOR

Number audit

Deterministic code: every value a lab claims must appear in that lab's own tool outputs.

4 · REVIEWER

Video + vision review

The Reviewer watches years of satellite time-lapse in one pass, then inspects every flagged frame.

5 · REPLICATION

Re-measured

Key numbers are recomputed from raw imagery, independently of the lab's cache.

Problems become versioned procedures (SOPs) that every lab follows, and an error ledger the public can read.
